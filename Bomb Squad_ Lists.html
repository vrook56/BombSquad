Bomb Squad: Lists

A device is armed.
Read the code.
Cut the right wire.

Each module shows four wires, and each wire has a Python snippet. Work out what every snippet does, then cut the one wire that matches the instruction. Your score is the time left on the clock. Each miss costs 15 seconds, and four misses sets off the bomb. Lists edition: Level I is indexing and slicing, Level II is loops and list comprehensions, and every game pulls 5 modules at random from a bank of 50 per level, so no two games are the same.

Live class games run from your instructor's link. Practice mode works right here.
